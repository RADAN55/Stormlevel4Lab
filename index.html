The Level 4 Lab

Twelve ELPA21 tasks for grades 9–12, each built around one move that separates a Level 3 answer from a Level 4 answer. Not a practice test — a set of drills on the behavior the rubric is actually looking for.

The target

ELPA21 reports a student Proficient when the score profile reaches Level 4 or higher in every domain. One domain at Level 3 holds the whole profile back.

Where the lab puts its weight

Eight of the twelve tasks sit in Reading and Writing, the two domains that carry the most weight in Mississippi exit decisions and that stall the most Progressing students.

Passages, emails, and figures in this lab are original practice texts written for classroom use. Names, schools, dates, and figures inside them are invented and are not announcements.

Progress is saved in this browser only. It does not travel to another device and no one else can see it.
